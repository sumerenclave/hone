How it works

Four surfaces. One number that tells you you're getting better.

01 — ASSESS

Twenty hands to find your level.

Real spots across preflop, flop, turn, and river. You get a skill rating out of 100 and a leak radar showing exactly where you're losing money.

02 — DRILL

Practice the exact spots you get wrong.

Every answer is graded by a real poker engine — equity simulated live, EV computed, and a coach's note on what the better line was and the cue to look for next time.

03 — PLAY

Sit at a full table that grades you.

Six-max hands against opponents with genuinely different styles — tight, loose, aggressive, passive — with real betting, side pots, and showdowns.

04 — REVIEW

Replay any hand, street by street.

See your actual equity at every decision point, what the opponent's line meant, and which single decision cost you the most.

Honest math, no gimmicks

The math is never wrong.

Every verdict comes from a deterministic poker engine running on your phone — hand evaluation, Monte Carlo equity simulation, range modeling, and EV analysis. Nothing is hand-waved, and no number is ever invented.
