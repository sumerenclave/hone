Hone Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Hone is a poker training app made by Enclave HK Limited. This policy explains what data Hone handles. The short version: we collect nothing.

Data collection

Hone does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

  • No account. Hone has no sign-up, login, or user accounts.
  • No analytics or tracking. Hone contains no analytics SDKs, advertising
    • SDKs, or trackers of any kind.
  • No network transmission of your data. Your play history, skill rating,
    • drill results, and settings are stored only on your device and never leave it.

Your data, your device

All progress data lives in files on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no copy anywhere else.

Children

Hone is an educational app about poker strategy using play money only. It contains simulated gambling and is age-rated accordingly on the App Store. It does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version of Hone ever adds a feature that transmits data (for example, an online coaching service), this policy will be updated first, and the App Store privacy label will be updated to match.
