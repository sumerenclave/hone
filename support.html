Common questions

Where is my data stored? Entirely on your device. Hone has no accounts and no servers, so deleting the app deletes all progress permanently.

Can I reset my progress? Yes: delete and reinstall the app. There is nothing to reset server-side.

Is this real-money poker? No. Hone is an educational trainer using play money only. There is no gambling, no prizes, and no way to wager anything.

Why is the app rated 18+? App Store rules require an 18+ rating for any app that depicts simulated gambling, even educational trainers with no real money.
